How it's wired

The architecture

Home Assistant doesn't need a hub of its own like SmartThings does. It runs on any computer, and radio adapters plug into it (or sit on the network) to talk to Zigbee and Z-Wave devices.

What it actually does

Automations from daily life

Approach lights

After dark, when the cameras see a person on the driveway or at the door, the right outside light turns on. It turns off three minutes after they leave, unless someone switched it on by hand.

Garage on arrival

When a family member's phone arrives home, the garage door opens for them.

Doorbell face recognition

Frigate recognises family faces at the front door, so alerts can say who is there instead of just "person detected".

EV plug-in estimate

Plugging in the Tesla sends a phone notification with the expected time to reach the charge limit. On weekends it starts charging straight away instead of waiting for the overnight schedule.

Bathroom fans and timers

Shower humidity runs the exhaust fans. Picking up a toothbrush starts a spoken two-minute timer on the bathroom smart clock.

Spoken announcements

Announcements play on the Sonos and Google speakers. Music pauses for the announcement and resumes afterwards.

Network watchdog

Home Assistant watches the network switch and DNS server, and sends a quiet phone alert if a Wi-Fi access point, camera or the Zigbee coordinator drops off the network.

Tiered notifications

Alerts are sorted into critical, normal and quiet levels, so a leak or smoke alarm breaks through Do Not Disturb but routine updates never wake anyone.

Getting started doesn't need all of this

  • A Home Assistant Green plus a Zigbee/Thread dongle and a Z-Wave stick will run a typical SmartThings home.
  • Zigbee and Z-Wave devices from SmartThings pair directly to the new adapters. Re-pair them room by room and run both systems side by side during the move.
  • Wi-Fi devices like Tapo, Sonos, Hue and Google Cast are usually found automatically.
  • The Proxmox server, cameras and extra services here were added over time. They're optional.
